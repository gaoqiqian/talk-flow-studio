## Comparative acceptance test

Before claiming superior results, edit the same authorized 60-second source with this workflow and two named comparison products. Keep the same brief, supplied evidence and export resolution. Randomize labels and have at least three reviewers score story clarity, speech synchronization, information density, typography/layout, meaningful motion, evidence clarity and audio/export reliability from 0–2. Record time, cost, revisions, reviewer scores and failure cases. Do not use fabricated results or automatic screenshot checks as a substitute for this test.
